# Presentation: Spring Break on a Student Budget: New Jersey Edition

## S - Situation

As college students, we love the idea of spring break adventures, but with limited budgets, choosing an affordable destination in New Jersey can be tricky. Many popular shore spots seem out of reach, and we need a way to compare options realistically.

## T - Task

I was tasked with building an interactive data story app that helps students compare the affordability of New Jersey spring break destinations based on their budget, trip length, and lodging arrangements.

## A - Action

I developed a React TypeScript application using Vite for fast development and Recharts for data visualization. The app features three key views: a cost comparison bar chart, cost breakdown chart, and a distance vs cost scatter plot. I implemented interactive controls including a budget slider, trip length selector, and lodging toggle. Data transformations calculate affordability in real-time, and I added annotations to highlight key insights.

## R - Result

The app reveals that sharing lodging with 4 friends can reduce costs by up to 75%, making destinations like Atlantic City affordable for $500 budgets on 3-day trips. With interactions, users see that 50% more destinations become accessible when switching to shared lodging. One limitation is the use of estimated data, but this provides a solid foundation for budget planning. Actionable takeaway: Always check lodging sharing options – it can turn an impossible trip into an affordable reality.
